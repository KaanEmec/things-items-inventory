# Phone guide

The phone app is the web app, installed to your home screen. It needs HTTPS
on your own domain (see [SELF-HOSTING.md](SELF-HOSTING.md)), because the camera
and "install" only work over HTTPS.

## Install on iPhone (iOS)

1. Open your Stash address in Safari. Other iOS browsers cannot install web apps.
2. Tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open it from the home screen. It runs full screen, like an app.

## Install on Android

1. Open your address in Chrome.
2. Open the menu and tap **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
3. Open it from the home screen.

On Android you can also share photos from the gallery to the app.

## Keep the app open to finish uploading

Photos you take are saved on the phone first and then uploaded from a queue. The queue shows "Keep the app open to finish uploading" while photos are
waiting. Leave the app open, on a screen you can see, until the queue is empty.

If the browser cannot keep photos on the device, the app warns you. Photos in
that state are lost if you close the app before they upload.

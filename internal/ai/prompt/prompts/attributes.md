Attributes
- Use only the keys the <household> block lists for the answer's category, each at most once; leave out keys you have nothing for. Reason: the app rejects keys that don't belong to the category.
- Values are text. Numbers are bare and in the unit the key names: "256" for storage_gb, "15.6" for screen_in, "1.5" for length_m; convert only exact units (1 TB is "1024"). Listed values are written exactly as listed ("usb_c", "spring_autumn"). For "any of" keys, separate several values with commas ("usb_c, bluetooth"). Reason: the app converts values to numbers and lists and rejects anything else.
- Free-text attributes (power, rating, size, material, care) are copied as printed ("19 V ⎓ 3.42 A", "42", "80% cotton, 20% polyester").
